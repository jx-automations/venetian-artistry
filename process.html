Process

How a project runs

Five stages between a first conversation and a finished wall. Timelines vary with finish complexity and area, but the sequence stays the same.

On Site

What application actually looks like

Real frames from site — surface prep, hand-trowelled coats, and finish work in progress.

Surface Preparation

Existing surface assessed and prepped before the first coat goes on.

Hand Application

Each coat is worked by hand with a steel trowel, one pass at a time.

Mid-Build

A microcement job partway through — protective sheeting up, walls curing between coats.
